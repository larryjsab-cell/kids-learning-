# TiniLearners: product brief

**What it is.** Online store selling playful learning books for preschool to 1st grade (ages 3–7).

**Who it's for.** Parents and caregivers who want short, structured, screen-free learning at home.

**What's sold**
- **PDF download.** Delivered by email right after checkout.
- **Printed book + PDF.** The book ships, and the PDF is emailed straight away.
- **TiniLearners Club (monthly).** Unlocks every PDF, plus each new PDF release free on launch day.

**Checkout.** Stripe embedded checkout in a slide-out drawer (Phase 3).

**Lead magnet.** Free sample pack (5 pages) in exchange for an email address.

**Contact.** tinilearners@gmail.com. Online store with no phone and no hours.

**Design direction.** Playful kid design. It's aimed at parents, but delightful to kids.
- **Signature.** Handwriting-paper rules (top line, dashed midline, coral baseline) behind key headings, and a letter that traces itself once in the hero.
- **Palette.** Grape primary, sunshine, mint and coral fills, sky surface, ink text.
- **Type.** Grandstander for display, Lexend for body. Lexend was designed for reading fluency.
- **Buttons.** Chunky "sticker" buttons with an ink offset shadow that press down on click.
- **Voice.** Plain, warm, sentence case. Uses numerals.

**Reference.** IM8 Essentials Pro product page (premium DTC landing structure), translated for kids.
